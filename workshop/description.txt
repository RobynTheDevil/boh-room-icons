Hush House will tell you what a locked room costs, but only if you hover it and wait, or open its detail window one room at a time. Room Icons puts the answer on the map.

Every locked room you can reach shows its unlock requirements as aspect icons, drawn on the room itself. You can see a route through the house instead of discovering it a door at a time.

[b]Reading the icons[/b]
[list]
[*][b]A solid group[/b] is the essential aspects. You need all of them.
[*][b]A dot-separated group[/b] is the required principles. Any one of them, at its listed strength, is enough.
[/list]

The icons hold their size on screen as you zoom out, so the whole house stays readable from any distance, and a room's icons disappear the moment it opens.

[b]Requires Ghirbi, the Gatekeeper[/b]
Room Icons is a DLL mod and will not load on its own. Subscribe to Ghirbi as well, or nothing will happen.
